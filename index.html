Helpful answers

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before getting started with ShaloTrack.

How does installation work?

A ShaloTrack technician can install the GPS device at your location or service point. Installation is handled professionally, then the device is activated and connected to your account.

Which vehicles can be tracked?

ShaloTrack supports cars, SUVs, motorcycles, scooters, three-wheelers, vans, lorries, buses, heavy vehicles and multi-vehicle fleets. Contact us if you need guidance on the right setup.

Can I manage more than one vehicle?

Yes. Our platform is designed to show multiple vehicles in one account, helping owners and businesses see fleet activity, vehicle status and alerts from one place.

What happens if a device goes offline?

Your account can show the device's most recent information and alert you to an offline status, so you can follow up quickly with the right next step.
