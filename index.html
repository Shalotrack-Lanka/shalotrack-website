07 — Questions

Answered plainly.

How does installation work?

A technician fits the device at your location or our service point. It is wired in, activated and linked to your account before they leave. Most installations take under an hour.

Which vehicles can be tracked?

Cars, SUVs, motorcycles, scooters, three-wheelers, vans, lorries, buses, heavy vehicles and mixed fleets. If you are unsure what suits yours, send the details and we advise before quoting.

How do I sign in?

With your phone number. We send a one-time code by SMS, so there is no password to forget or leak. Email verification is available as a second option.

Can I manage more than one vehicle?

Yes. One account holds as many vehicles as you need, and you can share live tracking for a single vehicle with family or staff without giving away access to the rest.

What happens if a device goes offline?

Your account shows the last confirmed position and time, and flags the vehicle as offline so you can follow up. Usually it is underground parking, a disconnected power feed, or an area with no mobile coverage.
