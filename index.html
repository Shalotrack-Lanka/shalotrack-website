FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before getting started with ShaloTrack.

How does GPS tracker installation work?▼
Our certified technician visits your location or you bring your vehicle to our service point. The GPS device is professionally installed inside your vehicle — hidden and secure. Installation typically takes 30–60 minutes. We then activate the device and connect it to your mobile app before you leave.
Which vehicles are supported?▼
ShaloTrack supports all types of vehicles including cars, SUVs, motorcycles, scooters, three-wheelers, vans, lorries, trucks, buses, and heavy vehicles. We also support asset tracking for non-motorized equipment. If you're unsure, just contact us and we'll advise the right solution.
How long is the subscription period?▼
We offer flexible subscription plans — typically monthly or annual. Annual plans come with a discount compared to monthly billing. Your subscription covers the cloud platform, data transmission, mobile app access, and ongoing support. Renewals are easy and can be done directly through our team via WhatsApp.
Does the app work without internet on the phone?▼
The ShaloTrack mobile app requires an internet connection (mobile data or WiFi) to display live tracking data and receive alerts. The GPS device in your vehicle uses its own SIM card to transmit location data to our servers — so your vehicle is always tracked even if you're offline, and data is available once you reconnect.
Can I track multiple vehicles from one account?▼
Yes. Our Business and Fleet plans support multiple vehicles under a single account. You can view all vehicles on the map simultaneously, switch between them, and receive alerts for any vehicle in your fleet. Contact us for pricing based on the number of vehicles you need to track.
What happens if my GPS device goes offline?▼
You'll receive an instant push notification on your phone when any device goes offline. This could indicate a power cut, SIM issue, or tampering attempt. The app will show the last known location and timestamp. Our support team is available to help diagnose and resolve any device issues quickly.
Is my vehicle data private and secure?▼
Absolutely. All tracking data is encrypted and stored securely on our cloud infrastructure (Microsoft Azure). Only you and authorized users can access your vehicle's data. We never share or sell customer data to third parties. Your account is protected with secure authentication.
How do I get support if I have an issue?▼
Our local Sri Lankan support team is available via WhatsApp (+94 70 168 8777) and phone from 8 AM to 8 PM, seven days a week. For technical issues, we aim to respond within the hour. You can also email us at aloka@shalotrack.com for non-urgent queries.
