Before publishing this framework

Confirm the actual device warranty duration, installation coverage, exclusions, support hours, response commitments, replacement process, and any service-level promises. Do not publish a warranty or support claim until it has been approved by the business.

1. Warranty information to confirm

  • Device warranty coverage, start date, duration, and proof-of-purchase requirements.
  • Installation workmanship coverage and the conditions that may affect it.
  • Exclusions, including accident damage, unauthorized modification, vehicle electrical issues, water damage, misuse, or third-party work.
  • Assessment, repair, replacement, return, and any associated customer costs.

2. Support process to confirm

  • Which channels customers can use for installation, platform, renewal, billing, or technical support.
  • Published support hours, expected response handling, escalation route, and emergency procedure.
  • Information customers should provide when requesting support, such as account, vehicle, device, and issue details.
