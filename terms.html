Before publishing these terms

Confirm the exact commercial, technical, warranty, payment, subscription, cancellation, and dispute terms with the business owner and qualified legal reviewer. This page is not a binding agreement or legal advice.

1. Service scope and quotation

  • Define which products, devices, platform access, installation services, and support are included in each customer quote.
  • Confirm how quotes are accepted, how long they remain valid, and which changes may affect the final price.
  • State who is responsible for vehicle readiness, access, and any required approvals at the installation location.

2. Payment, subscription, and renewal

  • Confirm all payment schedules, accepted methods, taxes, deposits, late-payment treatment, and refund rules.
  • Set the subscription period, renewal notice, suspension process, and the customer steps required to renew or cancel.
  • Clarify what platform access and services remain available when a subscription ends.

3. Device use and account responsibilities

  • Define permitted use, safe operation requirements, account credentials, authorized users, and prohibited use cases.
  • State the customer responsibilities for vehicle ownership, lawful tracking use, SIM/device power, and communication availability.
  • Confirm the real operational limits of GPS, mobile-network coverage, map data, alerts, and remote vehicle functions.

4. Support, liability, and governing terms

The final terms should identify applicable support channels, warranty rules, reasonable service expectations, limitations, dispute process, governing law, and the legal entity providing the service.
