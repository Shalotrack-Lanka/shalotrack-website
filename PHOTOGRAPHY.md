# Photography — read this before launch

The design is photography-led. Right now it uses six free-licence Unsplash
photos hot-linked from `images.unsplash.com`. They render immediately so you
can see the real design, and the Unsplash licence permits commercial use with
no attribution required.

## Before you go live, do two things

**1. Download and self-host.** Hot-linking a third-party CDN on a production
site is a dependency you don't control. Save each image into `IMG/`, then
swap the `src` values in `index.html`. Also add `width` and `height`
attributes so the browser reserves space and the page stops jumping as
images load.

**2. Replace them with your own.** Generic road photography is a placeholder,
not a brand. Real ShaloTrack photos will beat any stock image here.

## Two more worth adding

- **Nuwan Aloka's portrait** replaces the orange "NA" monogram in the People
  section. A real face outperforms a graphic tile every time. Square, 1:1.
- **Team portraits** replace the four coloured monogram squares. Shoot all
  four the same way — same background, same light, same crop — or leave the
  monograms, which are at least consistent. A mix of real photos and
  monograms looks worse than either.

Square crops, 1:1, and keep the eyes on roughly the same line across all four.
