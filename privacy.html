Before publishing this notice

Confirm the actual data practices, retention periods, storage locations, access controls, third-party services, customer rights process, and the person responsible for privacy requests. This framework is not a complete privacy policy or legal advice.

1. Website enquiry and booking information

The current website form prepares a WhatsApp message containing the information a visitor enters. The static website does not submit that form to a separate ShaloTrack website server.

  • Information entered by a visitor: name, phone number, email, vehicle type, solution interest, preferred installation details, and message.
  • Purpose to confirm: replying to enquiries, preparing quotes, and arranging installation or support.
  • Communication channel: explain how messages sent through WhatsApp are handled and who can access them.

2. Information practices to confirm

  • Whether any website analytics, cookies, advertising tools, or embedded services collect information.
  • How vehicle-location, account, device, and support information is processed after a customer becomes active.
  • Where information is stored, which service providers are involved, and how long each category is retained.
  • What security safeguards and internal access controls are applied in practice.

3. Customer rights and requests

The final notice should explain how a person can ask questions, update information, request access, or raise a concern, along with the verified contact responsible for handling such requests.
